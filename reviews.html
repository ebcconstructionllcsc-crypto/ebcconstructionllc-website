Read full review

About four months ago, Edgar and his crew installed the driveway at my home. At the time, they were working alongside another company. I was initially concerned about hiring a team I didn’t know, especially with so many stories about scams and poor workmanship these days. It can be difficult to know whom to trust. They earned my confidence through their work, attention, and professionalism.

Edgar coordinated the entire project and stayed on top of the details. Although he is young, he demonstrated experience and leadership. Four months later, I’m still very happy with the results, and they will be the ones handling my next project.

Finding them again took some time. When I first met them, they didn’t have business cards or company logos yet, and I couldn’t remember how I had saved their number. But I remembered asking Edgar why he named his company EBC. He explained that he wanted a name that was easy to remember, similar to the alphabet. Interestingly, today I heard my niece practicing the alphabet, and that conversation came back to me. That’s how I found them again!

I’m happy to see how much EBC Construction LLC has grown in such a short time. Having seen how they work, their growth doesn’t surprise me. The company may be newer, but the experience and dedication they demonstrated on my property were clear.

I’m sharing a photo of the driveway they completed at my home. My recommendation comes from personal experience: they did excellent work and gave me every reason to hire them again. I highly recommend EBC Construction LLC, and I especially thank Edgar for his attention and for coordinating our project so well.

Yuiuh GrayGoogle · ★★★★★
