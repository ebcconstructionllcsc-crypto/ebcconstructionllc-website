What we review before building

What should I send for an estimate?

Send the address, approximate wall length, photos from both sides and any plans you already have. Show the slope, driveway, patio and buildings near the proposed wall.

Can a wall be included with concrete or landscaping?

Yes. Include every area in your request so we can review the elevations, drainage and order of work together. The estimate will define what is included.

Does the wall need plans?

Requirements depend on the site and the wall. We review any engineering plans, permits and project requirements that apply before agreeing on construction.
