Frequently asked questions

Thinking about a project? Start here.

A few quick answers can make your next step easier.

What kind of projects does EBC take on?

Concrete is our main service. We also work on pavers, landscaping, grading and excavation, porches and decks, light remodeling, and welding.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. Share the project address, the type of work and a few details so we can review it and coordinate the next step.

What should I send to get started?

Send the address, approximate dimensions, a short description of what you want done and any photos you have. That helps us understand the project before we talk through next steps.

Can I send photos instead of scheduling a visit first?

Yes. Photos are a helpful way to start. We may need to review the site and conditions to confirm the project details.

What areas do you serve?

Serving Greenville, Greer, Spartanburg and Upstate South Carolina. Send your project address to confirm availability.

Can I ask about more than one service?

Absolutely. Tell us everything you would like to improve so we can understand how the work may fit together.

What happens after I send my project details?

We review the information you share and follow up to discuss the project and coordinate the next step.

How do I request an estimate?

Use the estimate form, call or text us. Include the best way and time to reach you, and we will follow up about the project.
