Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before work begins.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. Send your location, project type, approximate size and photos so we can review the scope and arrange the next step.

Can I send project photos?

Yes. The estimate form accepts multiple project photos so our team can better understand access, conditions and scope.

What does the warranty cover?

Coverage depends on the service, materials and site conditions. Applicable coverage is explained in writing before the project begins.

Do you work with homeowners and contractors?

Yes. EBC supports residential clients, builders, remodelers and contractors with concrete, grading, excavation and related site work.
