01

Getting started

A few details are enough to start a conversation.

Is the estimate free?

Yes. EBC offers free estimates. Tell us what you want to build and where the property is so we can review your request and arrange the next step.

What should I have ready?

Start with the project address, a description of the work and a phone number where we can reach you. Photos and approximate dimensions help. If you have not measured the space, describe what you know and include your questions.

Do you work in my area?

We are based in Greer and serve Upstate South Carolina, western North Carolina and northeast Georgia. Send the project address so we can confirm availability for your location.

Explore our service areas →
02

Planning the work

Understand what shapes the scope and schedule.

What affects the price of a concrete project?

Size, access, existing surfaces, preparation, drainage and the chosen finish all matter. We review the conditions at your property before confirming the scope and estimate. Include photos of the area and any access or drainage concerns.

Can you extend an existing driveway or patio?

We review the existing surface, available space and drainage to plan the extension. Tell us how you want to use the new area and share photos showing where it would connect to the existing concrete.

When can my project begin?

Include your preferred timing in the request. We discuss availability, site readiness and weather when planning the work. Sending a request does not reserve a start date; the schedule is confirmed with you.

03

What happens next

Know what to expect after you send your details.

What happens after I request an estimate?

We review the information you share, contact you to discuss the project and coordinate the next step. If a site visit is needed, we arrange it with you. Include any questions and a convenient time to reach you.

How do I know my request was sent?

After pressing Send request to EBC, wait for the confirmation message on the page. Reviewing your details alone does not send them. If you see an error or no confirmation, keep your details and contact us to check before sending again.

Contact EBC →
When can I use the new concrete?

Ask the EBC team for the instructions that apply to your project before walking, driving or placing heavy items on the new concrete. Timing depends on the work and conditions; confirm it with the team instead of relying on a general number of days.
