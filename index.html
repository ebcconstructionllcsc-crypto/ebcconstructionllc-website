Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before work begins.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. Send the project address, service type, approximate size and photos so EBC can review the scope and arrange the next step.

What services does EBC perform?

EBC provides concrete, grading, excavation, landscaping and selected remodeling services.

Does EBC provide a warranty?

Yes. Applicable warranty coverage is explained according to the service, materials, site conditions and written project scope before work begins.

Can I send photos of my project?

Yes. The estimate form accepts multiple photos so EBC can better understand access, conditions and the work requested.

What areas do you serve?

EBC is based in Greer and serves Upstate South Carolina, Western North Carolina, the Charlotte area and North Georgia. Send the address to confirm availability.
